If you or someone you know is in immediate danger, call 911. For mental health or substance use crisis support, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) — available 24/7, free, and confidential. You can also reach our team directly at (844) 975-4673.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get access to the app?

The Milton Nation Alumni App is available exclusively to Milton Recovery Centers alumni and staff. After you download the app and submit your registration, a staff member will review and approve your account — usually within 24 hours. You'll receive a push notification the moment you're approved.

I'm not receiving my login verification text

Make sure the phone number on your profile matches the number you're checking. Verify your phone has signal and isn't blocking texts from unknown numbers. The code expires after 60 seconds — tap "Resend" to get a new one. If the issue persists, contact us below.

I forgot my password

On the login screen, tap "Forgot Password" to receive a reset link to your email address. Check your spam or junk folder if you don't see it within a few minutes. If you still can't log in, contact us and we'll help you regain access.

My account is pending — I can't access the app

New accounts are reviewed by Milton Recovery Centers staff to verify alumni status. If your account has been pending for more than 24 hours, reach out to us directly and we'll get you approved as quickly as possible.

How do I update my phone number or profile?

Inside the app, go to your Profile → Edit Profile to update your name, phone number, profile photo, and other information. Your updated phone number will be used for future login verification codes.

How do I find nearby AA/NA meetings?

Tap the Meetings tab at the bottom of the app, then tap Nearby Meetings. Allow location access when prompted. Meetings near you will appear on a map and as a list. You can also search by address or browse all meetings.

How do I turn off notifications?

Go to your iPhone Settings → Notifications → Milton Nation to turn off all notifications, or manage which types you receive. You can also control notification preferences inside the app under Settings → Notifications.

How does sobriety tracking work?

The app automatically calculates your sobriety milestone based on the sobriety date you entered during registration. You earn badges at key milestones (30 days, 60 days, 90 days, 6 months, 1 year, and beyond). You can update your sobriety date at any time from your Profile.

Is my information private?

Yes. The Milton Nation Alumni App is a closed, private community — only approved Milton Recovery Centers alumni and staff can see content inside the app. Your recovery information (sobriety date, program type) is never shared with insurance companies, employers, or any third party. Read our full Privacy Policy for details.

How do I report a post or message?

To report inappropriate content, press and hold on any post or message and tap Report. Our moderation team reviews all reports promptly. In the case of a mental health emergency, crisis resources are automatically surfaced and staff are alerted.

I want to delete my account

You can request account deletion from inside the app under Settings → Delete My Account, or email us at admissions@miltonrecovery.com. We'll delete your account and all associated data within 30 days.

I found a bug or something isn't working

We appreciate you letting us know. Email us at admissions@miltonrecovery.com with a description of what happened and what you expected to happen. Screenshots are very helpful if you're able to share them.

Is the app free?

Yes. The Milton Nation Alumni App is completely free for all Milton Recovery Centers alumni. There are no in-app purchases, subscriptions, or hidden fees.
