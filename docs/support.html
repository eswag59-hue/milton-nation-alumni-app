Frequently Asked Questions

I'm not receiving my login verification text

Make sure the phone number on your profile matches the number you're checking. Check that your phone has signal and isn't blocking texts from unknown numbers. The code expires after 60 seconds — tap "Resend" to get a new one. If the issue persists, contact us below.

I forgot my password

On the login screen, tap "Forgot Password" to receive a reset link to your email address. Check your spam folder if you don't see it within a few minutes.

My account is pending / I can't access the app

New accounts are reviewed by Milton Recovery Centers staff to verify alumni status. If your account is pending, contact us directly and we'll get you approved as soon as possible.

How do I update my phone number?

Go to your Profile → Edit Profile and update your phone number. Your new number will be used for future login verification codes. Contact support if you're locked out.

How do I find nearby AA/NA meetings?

Tap the Meetings tab and then tap "Nearby Meetings." Allow location access when prompted. Meetings near you will appear on the map and in a list below.

How do I turn off notifications?

Go to your iPhone Settings → Notifications → Milton Nation and adjust your notification preferences there. You can also manage some notification settings within the app under Settings.

I want to delete my account

Email us at ezra@miltonrecoverycenters.com with your request. We'll delete your account and all associated data within 30 days.

I found a bug or something isn't working

We appreciate you letting us know. Email us with a description of what happened and what you expected to happen. Screenshots are helpful if you can share them.

Is the app free?

Yes. The Milton Nation Alumni App is completely free for all Milton Recovery Centers alumni. There are no in-app purchases or subscriptions.
