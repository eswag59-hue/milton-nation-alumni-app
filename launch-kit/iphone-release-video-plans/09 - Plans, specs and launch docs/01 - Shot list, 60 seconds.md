# 60-second launch film — locked treatment and shot list

Every creative decision below was confirmed by Ezra on 2026-08-25, not assumed.
The Higgsfield prompts are final and ready to fire in order.

## Locked decisions

| Decision | Locked to |
|---|---|
| Emotional arc | Quiet → kinetic → quiet |
| Story spine | One person, day 1 → day 1,096. Loneliness→Milton opens it; the five tabs are the beats in Act III; the care team is the emotional core |
| Audio | Music + kinetic type. **No voiceover** — it has to work muted in feed |
| Motion scale | Escalates through all four: subtle → layered lift-off → full deconstruction → abstract → back to subtle |
| People on screen | **Hands and light only.** No identifiable face, ever. No one can be mistaken for a real member |
| Tagline | **Every day, counted.** |
| Music | Solo piano → strings → full swell → back to solo piano. Closes the circle |
| Sequencing | Film first, App Store listing replacement immediately after |

**Type throughline:**
> Day one is the loneliest day. → You're not doing this alone. → Every day, counted. → 1,096 days.

## Why "hands and light only" is a hard rule

This is a recovery app. An AI-generated face in this film invites the question
"is that a real patient?" — which is a question you never want asked. Hands,
shoulders, glow on a turned-away face: intimate, real, and unmistakably not a
testimonial. Every prompt below enforces it explicitly.

### Act I — Alone

**S01 · 0:00–0:06 · the dark room**
> Extreme low-key cinematic shot. A smartphone lies face-down on a wooden nightstand in a completely dark bedroom, deep night. A single distant teal point of light glows far behind, heavily out of focus. Almost everything is black — we read shape, not detail. Volumetric darkness, faint dust. Static locked-off camera, imperceptible slow drift forward. Shot on a fast prime, very shallow depth of field, deep crushed blacks, fine grain. No people, no faces, no hands, no text, no logos.

**S02 · 0:06–0:12 · it lights up** **[SCREEN]**
> The phone on the nightstand lights up in the dark, screen glow spilling across the wood grain and up onto the edge of a shoulder turned away from camera. We never see a face — only the rim of a shoulder and the suggestion of someone lying still. The screen shows the provided reference interface exactly as given, unaltered. Slow push toward the glow. Warm-cool contrast between the screen's light and the black room. Cinematic, intimate, restrained. No visible face, no eyes, no identifiable person, no text overlays, no logos.

### Act II — The reach

**S03 · 0:12–0:17 · the tap** **[SCREEN]**

The screen displays the provided reference interface exactly as given, unaltered and legible. The moment is quiet and deliberate. Shallow focus on the fingertip. No face, no arm above the wrist, no identifiable person, no text, no logos.

No people, no hands, no new text, no logos.

### Act III — The world opens

The particles carry the brand colours — deep teal, cyan, soft lime. Camera pushes through the dissolving form into the dark beyond. Epic, cinematic, high production value, like a flagship keynote transition. Deep blacks, volumetric light shafts, lens bloom. No people, no faces, no text, no logos.

Teal, cyan and lime accents against near-black. Fast, kinetic, exhilarating, cinematic. No people, no faces, no readable new text, no logos.

**S07 · 0:34–0:40 · the counter**
> A colossal numeric counter stands forty feet tall in a dark volumetric space, its digits made of clean glowing light, rolling upward at speed through increasing values. Camera cranes up alongside the digits, low angle, monumental scale. A ribbon of teal-to-cyan-to-lime light sweeps through the space behind it. Epic, architectural, cinematic, keynote-grade. Deep blacks, atmospheric haze, anamorphic bloom. No people, no faces, no words, no logos.

### Act IV — The peak

**S08 · 0:40–0:45 · collapse to light**
> Every element in frame collapses inward into a single horizontal band of pure light spanning the frame, a gradient running deep teal through cyan into soft lime. The surrounding space is absolute black. The band pulses once, softly, and begins to settle. Pure abstract motion graphics, no objects, no structure. Immaculate, minimal, expensive. No people, no text, no logos.

**S09 · 0:45–0:50 · the horizon**
> The horizontal band of light slowly resolves into a real horizon: sunrise over still water, low mist sitting on the surface, seen from a quiet shoreline. The transition is seamless — abstract light becoming a real dawn. Warm golden sun breaking, natural colour, gentle, hopeful, understated. Handheld stillness, fine grain, shot like a real photograph rather than a render. No people, no boats, no text, no logos.

### Act V — Landing

Calm, warm, domestic, quiet. Natural light, shallow depth of field, fine grain. No people, no hands, no faces, no text overlays, no logos.
