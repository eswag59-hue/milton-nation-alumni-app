# Milton Nation — Video Scripts (full launch slate)

12 videos. Each script has: purpose, runtime, visuals column (maps to clips in `02-RECORDING-SHOT-LISTS.md`), voiceover (VO), and on-screen text (OST). Voice matches the in-app copy bank: warm, direct, never clinical.

**Platform variants:** every video ends on an end-card. Ship two versions of the end-card only — the body of each video is identical.
- **iOS end-card:** app icon + wordmark + official Apple **"Download on the App Store"** badge.
- **Android end-card (for when Android ships):** same + official **"Get it on Google Play"** badge. Until an Android build exists, use "Android — coming soon" instead of a Play badge. *(Never show a Play badge for an app that isn't on Play.)*

**Music direction (all videos):** organic, warm, mid-tempo. Piano/soft synth pads building to a gentle swell. No EDM drops, no melancholy strings. Think "sunrise," not "sad commercial" and not "tech keynote."

---
