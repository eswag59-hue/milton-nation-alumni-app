1

Fix SMS Delivery

Likely: A2P 10DLC registration (US carrier requirement)

10 min + 1–3 day wait

Skip this phase if Step 0.4 returned "SMS arrived" — you're already good. Otherwise:

Background: Why SMS might be silently dropped

US carriers (AT&T, Verizon, T-Mobile) require app-to-person SMS senders to register their "brand" and "campaign" under A2P 10DLC rules. Without registration, carriers silently drop messages — no error returned to you, no failed-delivery log. The Edge Function thinks it succeeded; the user just never receives anything. Fix: register the brand + campaign with Twilio, who relays it to the carriers.

If already paid: Skip this step.
